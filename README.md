# Effects of GDP on Access to Renewable Energy
Data story 1

Sustainable development has three major aspects: **economic development**, **broad-based social inclusion**, and **environmental sustainability**. Within these broad aspects, sustainible developmnet goal number 7 is to “ensure access to affordable, sustainable modern energy.” With this and the broad aspects of sustainable development in mind, **countries with a low GDP should be experiencing the transition to new forms of renewable, clean energy at the same rates as countries with high GDPs.** This data explores whether the percentage of total energy that is renewable is influenced by a country's GDP.
